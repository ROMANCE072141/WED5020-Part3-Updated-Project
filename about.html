About YEAST

Our purpose is built around quality products and a welcoming community experience.

Organisation overview

YEAST Artisan Bakery & Grocer is presented as a local bakery and grocery business for this WED5020 student project. The website demonstrates accessible navigation, product information, interactive functionality and search engine optimisation.

To provide quality baked goods and selected grocery products while creating a welcoming community experience.

To become a trusted local destination for fresh bakery products, everyday groceries and friendly service.

Care, consistency, clear communication and a positive customer experience guide the way this project presents the organisation.

Project note: This is a portfolio demonstration. Business claims, contact details and physical locations should be verified before the website is used commercially.
